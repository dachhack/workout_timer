Photos bundled with the app and shown behind the splash message.

Any .jpg, .jpeg, .png, or .webp in this folder is picked up automatically —
no code changes, just rebuild. They are pooled with any photos added from the
phone's gallery (the camera icon on the home screen), and one of the whole set
is chosen at random each time the app opens.

Keep these modest: they ship inside the APK. Roughly 1920px on the long edge
at JPEG quality 85 looks right on a phone screen and costs a few hundred KB.
Portrait shots fill the screen best, since the photo is cropped to fill.
